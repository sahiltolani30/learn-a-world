# MOSAIC Research Evidence Ledger

**Research checkpoint:** 2026-11-04
**Scope:** visual-only learning agent for Crafter, with transfer experiments to other interactive simulators
**Integrity rule:** a paper result is not treated as a Crafter result unless the source actually evaluates Crafter or the claim is explicitly marked as transfer reasoning.

## Evidence status vocabulary

- **Verified-local:** directly read or executed in this repository/runtime.
- **Repository-reported:** recorded by project artifacts, but not independently rerun in this runtime.
- **Primary-source:** stated in the cited paper or official implementation.
- **Reasoned:** implication drawn from evidence; requires a targeted experiment.
- **Unknown:** not established by available evidence.

## Evidence gaps that block a final empirical claim

1. No independently reproduced Crafter training run from the current checkout was possible in this runtime.
2. No measured T4/Colab throughput, memory profile, or environment-worker sweep exists in this checkpoint.
3. No experiment yet establishes that a learned relation/event ledger identifies hidden mechanics rather than correlating with reward or visual coincidence.
4. No experiment yet compares the proposed hybrid with a strong recurrent/object-centric PPO baseline under a single locked protocol.
5. No calibration experiment establishes that ensemble disagreement predicts held-out transition error in the learned visual belief space.
6. No transfer experiment establishes persistent memory usefulness across episode resets or unseen object appearances.
